Refund Policy

How refunds work for Tessera subscriptions and one-time usage top-ups.

Effective July 22, 2026 · Authority Index Labs, Inc.

Plain language is intentional. This policy tells you exactly what qualifies for a refund, what does not, and how to ask for one.

Where you bought Tessera decides who handles your refund

Apple App Store purchases (iOS)

If you subscribed or bought a top-up inside the Tessera iOS app, Apple processes the payment and Apple handles all refunds. We do not receive your payment and cannot issue, approve, or deny a refund for an App Store purchase.

To request an App Store refund, use Apple's own flow:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  3. Find the Tessera charge and choose the refund option.

Apple decides these requests under its own terms. If you need the purchase date or amount to complete Apple's form, email us and we will help you find it.

Purchases made on our website or in the Web App

If you subscribed or bought a top-up on our website or in the Tessera Web App, we process the payment and we handle refunds directly. The rest of this policy governs those purchases.

Subscriptions (Standard, Pro, Power)

Tessera subscriptions are Standard ($9.99/month), Pro ($19.99/month), and Power ($79.99/month). They renew automatically each month until cancelled.

What qualifies for a refund

We will refund a web subscription charge when any of the following is true:

  1. Billing error. You were charged the wrong amount, charged after you cancelled, or charged for a plan you did not select.
  2. Duplicate charge. You were charged more than once for the same subscription period.
  3. First-period goodwill window. You contact us within 14 days of your first paid charge on a web subscription and you have not substantially used that period's usage allowance. In that case we refund the charge in full.

What does not qualify for a refund

  • Usage you have already consumed during the billing period.
  • Reaching your monthly usage allowance or a daily usage ceiling. Hitting a limit is how the plans are designed to work and is not a basis for a refund.
  • Partial or unused portions of a billing period after the 14-day first-period window, including when you simply did not use Tessera that month.
  • Renewal charges after the first paid period. You can cancel anytime to stop future renewals, but a renewal that has already been charged is not refundable unless it is a billing error or duplicate charge.
  • Changing your mind after a free trial converts to a paid subscription, once you have used the paid period. To avoid a trial converting, cancel before the trial ends. We remind you by email before a trial converts.
  • Downgrades. Moving to a lower tier applies going forward and does not refund the current period.

How to stop future charges

Cancelling a subscription stops the next renewal. It does not by itself refund a charge that already happened.

  • Web App: Settings, then Subscription, then manage your plan.
  • iOS: manage the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

One-time usage top-ups

A usage top-up adds usage to your account immediately. Because the balance is available to spend the moment it is added, top-ups are treated as consumed on purchase.

We will refund a top-up only when:

  1. It is a billing error or a duplicate charge, or
  2. The top-up is completely unused. Once any part of a top-up has been used, it is non-refundable.

How to request a refund (web purchases)

Email support@authorityindexlabs.com from the email address on your Tessera account and include:

  • The date of the charge
  • The amount charged
  • Whether it was a subscription or a top-up
  • A short note on why you are requesting the refund

Requesting from your account email lets us verify the request quickly. If you cannot email from that address, tell us the account email you used.

Request window

  • First-period goodwill refunds: request within 14 days of your first paid charge.
  • Billing errors and duplicate charges: contact us as soon as you notice. We recommend within 60 days so your statement still shows the charge, but we will always correct a genuine billing error we can verify.

Our response time and how refunds are paid

  • Response: we will acknowledge and respond to a refund request within 5 business days.
  • Method: approved web refunds are issued to your original payment method. We do not issue cash, store credit, or refunds to a different card or account.
  • Timing: once approved, the refund is submitted right away. It typically appears on your statement within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first at support@authorityindexlabs.com. We can almost always resolve a genuine billing error faster than a bank dispute. Opening a chargeback before contacting us can lock the funds while the dispute runs and delay the outcome for both of us.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or remedy you are entitled to under the consumer protection laws that apply to you, including mandatory rights that cannot be waived. Where such a right applies, we honor it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version in force is the one published here on the date of your purchase. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
